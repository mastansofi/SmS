Frequently Asked Questions

How does the escrow payment guarantee work? expand_more

When you accept a worker’s bid, your funds are pre-authorized and held safely in an encrypted escrow vault. Funds are only transferred to the worker after you inspect the completed work and press "Confirm & Release".

Are workers on SmS verified? expand_more

Yes! Every tasker undergoes government photo ID validation and contact verification. Many carry additional background check clearance badges viewable on their profile.

What fees does SmS charge? expand_more

Posting tasks is 100% free. Posters pay a nominal payment processing protection fee upon completion. Workers pay a transparent low service tier which includes full insurance coverage.
